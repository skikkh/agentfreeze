# Code of conduct

AgentFreeze contributors must be respectful, precise, and safety-conscious.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, and coordinated
manipulation of community metrics are not accepted. Technical disagreement is welcome when it
addresses evidence and behavior rather than people.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation to protect
the project and its contributors.
